## On the radar

Some news items that caught tech workers’ attention this month:

- [Dutch workplaces aren’t prepared to offer safe working conditions amid rising heat](https://nltimes.nl/2026/07/11/dutch-workplaces-ready-rising-heat-labor-union-warns), labour union CNV concludes based on a survey of 1,500 on-site workers.
- “It is inherent in a system like AI that it wants you to become obsessed with it, whether in a positive or negative sense. We must prevent that.” Tech worker Olivia Guest and coworkers [criticize Radboud University’s offering of chatbot-driven writing course](https://www.voxweb.nl/en/scientists-critical-of-radbouds-into-languages-ai-courses-language-centre-emphasises-the-responsible-use-of-chatbots) that doesn’t meet academic standards, and undermines workers’ expertise.
- Workers in various industries in the Netherlands are withholding their labour to protest against government-proposed cuts to social security. On 3 July, [workers at large companies in the Eindhoven region, including ASML and DAF](https://www.bnr.nl/nieuws/economie/10605054/stakingsacties-bij-daf-en-asml-mensen-in-de-metaal-maken-zich-grote-zorgen), went on strike. [Transport workers are planning to do the same](https://www.nu.nl/economie/6404936/heel-openbaar-vervoer-gaat-plat-door-staking-op-woensdag-9-september.html) on 9 September.
- Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has [destroyed Amazon's central data hub in Bahrain in retaliation against US strikes on Iranian infrastructure](https://nitter.net/HormuzReport/status/2079449486527762876). The IRGC has warned 18 United States-based tech companies, including Microsoft, Intel, Cisco, and Google, that they’re legitimate targets.
- Workers at Wikimedia have unionized. [Wikimedia says it won’t voluntarily recognize the union.](https://www.404media.co/wikimedia-waits-until-after-its-massive-conference-to-say-it-wont-voluntarily-recognize-union/)
- “I strongly believe there are entire companies right now under heavy AI psychosis and it’s impossible to have rational conversations with them about it.” Nikhil Suresh on how [AI mania is eviscerating global decision-making](https://ludic.mataroa.blog/blog/ai-mania-is-eviscerating-global-decision-making).
- Lacking a moat to prevent their customers going elsewhere, vendors of closed-source LLM tools issue a [warning against using open-weights models](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/22/openai-anthropic-open-models-trump-china).
- Did you know that data centre capacity already [outstrips demand by factor 15](https://thenextrecession.wordpress.com/2026/07/23/ai-and-productivity/)? And still companies are preparing to build more of them in the Netherlands…
- How can information retrieval be a force for societal good? [Talk + slides](https://mastodon.social/@bmitra/117003838454044213) by Bhaskar Mitra encouraging workers in (AI-assisted) information retrieval to be more conscious of the political dimension of their work.
- Managers are mandating the use of a technology that many workers are actively avoid, or even sabotaging. Here’s some [tips from fellow tech workers on resisting LLM mandates](https://aftermath.site/ai-resistance-tips-workforce-llm-workers/).
- [New Dahabflex](https://youtu.be/MDcM5tN00VU?si=i3mn7idagmuVBxtz) just dropped!
